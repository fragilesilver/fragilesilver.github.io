The Jar & Shake Power Meter

Key Highlights

Dual SD Card & PortMaster Scan

Scans both SD1 and SD2 (ROMS/<system>/) plus PortMaster ports (ports/*.sh). Bundles sample sets when empty.

Weighted Shake Mechanics

Backlog titles get ×3 weight, favourites ×2, playing ×1.5, beaten ×0.5. Perfect shake timing gives an extra weighting boost!

In-App Genre Editor

Edit and adjust game genres right on the result screen without modifying your read-only gamelist.xml files.

Retro CRT Visuals & Audio

Procedurally synthesized retro sound effects paired with CRT scanlines, neon chromatic aberration, and arcade aesthetic.

Non-Intrusive Design

JarMu is strictly a game selector: it decides what to play so you can launch it cleanly through muOS yourself. This prevents core mismatches and ensures 100% stability across every RG35XX screen revision.
