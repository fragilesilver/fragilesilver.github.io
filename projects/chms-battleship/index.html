Classroom battle-royale Battleship with Cambridge IGCSE pseudocode

How the Battle Royale Works

Student crews share one massive communal ocean on the classroom projector. To fire shots and sink opponent fleets, students must solve real Cambridge IGCSE and O Level pseudocode questions covering trace tables, conditional logic, loops, and array manipulations.

01. For Students

Instant Phone / Laptop Join

Students scan a QR code from the projector, enter their callsign, pick a team, and play without needing logins or passwords.

02. For Teachers

Live Teacher Dashboard

Full orchestrator controls: configure team counts, fleet sizes, starting shots, storm radius intervals, and grant bonus shots.

03. For Classroom

Projector Arena View

Live spectator board projecting the shared ocean grid, active radar sweeps, incoming hits, sunken ships, and closing storm walls.

Specialist Crew Roles

Captain
Places the team fleet and assigns specialist jobs to the crew.
Gunner
The primary sharpshooter authorized to fire torpedoes and artillery.
Navigator
Solves pseudocode logic to unlock radar and sonar scans.
Scientist
Completes algorithms to recharge tactical airstrikes and countermeasures.
Crew
Earns communal shots and recommends coordinate targets to the gunner.

Cambridge-Style Question Bank

Questions evaluate fundamental computer science understanding directly aligned with syllabus guidelines:

DECLARE Total : INTEGER
Total <- 0
FOR Count <- 1 TO 5
    IF Count MOD 2 = 0 THEN
        Total <- Total + Count
    ENDIF
NEXT Count
OUTPUT Total
-- What is the final value of Total? (Answer unlocks 2 artillery shots)
